Why the toilet tank refills itself

The big idea

A float is a switch that measures the water: level down opens the valve, level up closes it.

You will be able to: say which part triggers the refill, why it stops at the right level, and which part to blame when a toilet runs.

In the figure below, the orange cup sliding on the fill valve is the float. Its height is the water level, and the valve opening is its only output. Where the idea breaks: a light switch is on or off, but this valve closes gradually, like a dimmer.

Numbers: 4.8 L per flush and a 30 to 60 s refill are sourced (see the footer). The 8.0 L full volume, the 3.2 L left after the flush and the 4 s flush are illustrative values for a typical tank.
