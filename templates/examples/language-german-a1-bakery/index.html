Beim Bäcker

The big idea

In German, the noun's gender picks its article, and after “Ich möchte” only masculine articles change.

You will be able to: order seven bakery items with the right article, and say “Ich möchte einen Kaffee” without thinking.

1. Seven words, each with its gender colour

Learn every noun with its article and plural, as one chunk. Blue is der, red is die, green is das. Click 🔊 to hear a word; Shift+click plays it slowly.

Careful: DWDS lists Croissant as das or der; this lesson uses das. Wiktionary also records regional das Brezel and das Kaffee; the standard forms are die Brezel and der Kaffee.

2. Which article after “Ich möchte”?

You say “Das ist ein Kuchen” (der Kuchen is masculine). Now you order one.

Only the blue words change. Die and das look the same in both sentences, and so do eine and ein.
